# NMLB — Version 6
## The Living League

Version 6 is the major daily-life release. The app now treats the league as a world that continues to have culture, conversation, memory and anticipation even when no game is active.

### V6 centerpiece: Living League Home
- New V6 League Life engine feeding the Dashboard and Fandom.
- Daily edition framing (morning, afternoon, primetime, late night).
- Today in the League briefing with season day, event, pennant-race pressure and league conversation.
- League Chaos and Drama meters based on recent close games, walk-offs, extra innings, streaks and race pressure.
- Previously On recap that combines recent finals with clinch/elimination consequences.
- Weekly Show with Team of the Week, rough-week club, Game of the Week and next-week setup.

### Recurring people and conversations
- Recurring fan characters built from the existing fan-club universe.
- Persistent identities, running jokes, follower counts, reputation and prediction accuracy presentation.
- Fan-to-fan conversation web with tags, replies, questions, receipts and recurring rivalry relationships.
- No replacement of the user-customized social phrase bank.

### City, fanbase and emotional life
- City Baseball Climate with separate Hope and Anxiety scores.
- Mood labels such as Championship Dreaming, Believing, Restless and Gallows Humor.
- Baseball-climate states such as Electric, Watchful, Hostile and White-Knuckle.

### Media ecosystem
- Recurring programming lanes: Around the Diamond, Numbers Game, Bleacher Noise, October Watch and The Night Cap.
- Existing outlets, podcasts and prediction receipts are connected into the V6 presentation layer.
- Clinches and eliminations can remain active story waves rather than disappearing after the standings marker changes.

### Schedule life
- Road Trip / Homestand context from recent games.
- Off-day event aftermath keeps concerts, movies, Fan Fest and other events in the conversation for several league days.
- Meaningful-games board highlights elimination and scoreboard-watch pressure late in the season.

### Ballpark life
- Evolving park-reputation presentation based on tracked home results and walk-offs.
- Home record, park legend and signature-game context are surfaced together.
- Existing unique park geometry remains unchanged.

### Memory and history
- Historical Echo cards connect current streaks, dramatic games and present standings to the growing archive.
- “You Had to Be There” treatment for dramatic recent finals.
- On This Day remains connected to the daily-life presentation.

### Foundation / safety
- Visible app branding restored to Version 6.0.
- V6 persistent fields added safely inside leagueExtras for future watchlists, commissioner notes, fan memory, event traditions and season time capsules.
- Existing calendar, ceremonies, Museum/Hall, News, Fandom, social phrase customizations, standings and live-game systems remain in place.

### Culture amplification
- Dramatic games can receive informal memory names such as walk-off, marathon, one-run and scoreboard-chaos classics.
- Eliminated teams enter Spoiler Mode instead of falling out of the narrative.
- Repeated off-day events can be recognized as new rituals, traditions in progress or established traditions.
- V6 culture awards track the loudest baseball city, most anxious fanbase, fan-account main character, ballpark drama leader and league nonsense index.
- A live Season Time Capsule summarizes the current top team, league mood, main fan character, featured event, hashtag and chaos level.
- Letters From the Stands provide occasional warm long-form fan voice alongside the usual jokes and arguments.

### Newsroom V6
- V6 Daily Edition sits above the existing News desk.
- City Desk compares hope and anxiety around the league.
- Programming Grid turns media shows into recurring properties instead of isolated flavor cards.
- Postseason Aftermath, Spoiler Desk and History Echoes keep major results alive beyond a single headline.

## V6.3 — NMLB Rebrand
V6.3 makes Nicky's Major League Baseball the identity of the entire app rather than only the merch store. The global shell, navigation and major league surfaces now use the NMLB purple/white/black identity and NMLB naming.

### League-wide life
- New NMLB Power Index blends record, recent form, run differential, streak and postseason status into a team-level danger ranking without adding player-entry work.
- Record Chase Drama promotes existing major player-record targets into HISTORY TONIGHT / ON THE DOORSTEP / CLOSING FAST coverage.
- NMLB Network gains a full daily programming grid, debate lead, breaking desk and connected league-life presentation.
- NMLB Films generates documentary concepts from archived championships, rivalries and memorable tracked games.
- NMLB Magazine produces a current issue/cover identity from the live league state.
- Newspaper Front Pages turn clinches, eliminations, classic games and major daily stories into local/national-style front pages.
- NMLB Business Report combines simulated attendance, market heat and merch demand so winning streaks, October and fan energy read like one living economy.
- Instant Classic and Heartbreak boards rank tracked games by drama/context signals.
- Franchise Trajectory labels clubs as rising, contender, powerhouse, dynasty, declining, rebuilding or steady using current plus archived team results.
- Schedule Storylines turn homestands and road trips into league narrative rather than simple schedule labels.

### Spectacle
- NMLB Awards Night is a full-screen category-by-category reveal presentation for finalized season awards. Award-race leaders remain the pre-show/nominee board until winners are actually entered.
- The Bracket receives NMLB Postseason and World Series takeover presentation.
- The season now develops an NMLB narrative timeline and an era-style season identity based on the tracked league state.
- League-wide spectacle headlines can elevate record-breaking moments, clinches and extreme chaos into temporary HISTORY / POSTSEASON / NMLB TODAY presentation.

### Identity
- Main branding is now NMLB / Nicky's Major League Baseball.
- Official colors: royal purple #1f0554, white #ffffff and black #000000, with gold reserved for major historical/championship spectacle.
- Navigation now uses NMLB Center, NMLB Network, League Almanac, Hall of Records, Pennant Race and NMLB Office language.
- Existing V6 Living League, social, calendar, Museum/Hall, merch and gameplay systems remain intact.

## V6.4 — NMLB Culture & Postseason Spectacle
V6.4 gives the NMLB a recognizable culture and prepares the entire product for the league's biggest postseason yet.

### League culture
- Adds formal NMLB traditions: Opening Day, Rivalry Week, Midseason Celebration, Pennant Race, Awards Night, Hall Weekend, Fan Appreciation Finale and the World Series.
- Establishes recurring NMLB Network personalities, signature voices and recurring segments such as The Power Board, Pressure Meter, Receipts, History Lesson and The Panic Button.
- Culture & Lore now surfaces league-wide traditions and Network personalities alongside existing fanbase, ballpark and rivalry culture.

### NMLB Center facelift
- Rebuilds the NMLB Center header as a league-headquarters command surface with current Power Index leader, season identity, prediction state and archive counts.
- Restyles League Intelligence, Storylines, Players & Awards, Records & Milestones, Snapshots and Museum surfaces under one coherent NMLB headquarters visual language.

### Postseason prediction culture
- NMLB Network begins formal postseason forecasts in Week 7.
- Week 8 becomes Final Calls.
- Once postseason play begins, official NMLB Network picks lock into league history and become future receipts instead of changing retroactively.
- Dashboard, News and Bracket all surface the same postseason prediction state.

### Bracket spectacle
- Rebuilds the postseason bracket around Nicky's Commissioner's Trophy.
- World Series receives a dedicated grand central stage.
- Series cards visually shift toward the team controlling the series and fully surrender to the winner once a series is clinched.
- Adds fake postseason sponsors plus NMLB Network, NMLB+ and NMLB Radio watch information to bracket series.
- Champion state lights the central trophy presentation with the winning club's identity.

### Dashboard & News postseason ramp
- Dashboard gains Postseason Central presentation during the stretch run and October.
- News gains Road to October prediction coverage and Culture Desk programming.
- Existing postseason aftermath, spoiler, receipts, history and social systems remain connected to the new presentation.


## V6.4.2 — Commissioner Game Corrections

- Added a Commissioner Game Corrections desk under NMLB Center → Snapshots.
- Finalized regular-season games can be deleted while reversing the associated W/L and run differential.
- Existing finals can be reassigned to the correct franchise game number without re-entering the score.
- The league calendar can be returned directly to a chosen game slot after an accidental skip.
- Last-final state is refreshed after corrections so Dashboard context follows the repaired game book.
- Player stats are intentionally left untouched by game deletion; the tool will not guess at stat rollbacks.

## V6.4.3 — Grand Championship Ceremonies

- Rebuilt World Series and League Championship ceremonies as full NMLB live events.
- Championship banners now rise from the bottom of the presentation toward the rafters.
- Added 18 Commissioner speech variants and 24 manager speech variants with stable per-ceremony selection.
- Added fan-voice montage stages so championship nights include the fanbase, not only league officials.
- World Series ceremonies now feature an expanded Nicky’s Commissioner’s Trophy reveal, heavier team-color confetti, rings presentation and fireworks finale.
- League championship ceremonies receive the same speech, banner-raise, fan and fireworks treatment while preserving World Series-only trophy/ring hardware.
- Added a shared Firebase-backed ceremony session: when the operator starts or advances a ceremony, connected viewers follow the same live stage.
- Completed ceremonies scheduled for the current game can be restarted as a Grand Ceremony; restart clears the prior completion and replaces it when the new ceremony finishes.
- Visible NMLB version updated to V6.4.3.


## V6.4.5 — Majestic Championship Ceremonies

Championship ceremonies are now designed as the largest live spectacles in the NMLB.

- World Series ceremonies expand to a 17-beat coronation with a two-part Commissioner address, two-part manager address, season reflection, two waves of fan voices, slow banner presentation, trophy introduction/reveal, ring introduction/reveal, and a full fireworks finale.
- League Championship ceremonies expand to an 11-beat pennant celebration with the Commissioner, fan voices, manager remarks, season reflection, slow banner presentation, team-color confetti, and fireworks while remaining intentionally one tier below the World Series.
- Commissioner, manager, and fan voice banks were expanded substantially so repeated ceremonies have more variety and better flow.
- Banner raises now take roughly eight seconds. The confetti waits until the banner reaches the rafters, then the celebration erupts.
- Confetti now uses the club's actual franchise primary and secondary colors and increases to a 260-piece championship storm for major reveal stages.
- World Series trophy reveals receive a second confetti storm and an expanded fireworks field; the final coronation receives the largest fireworks treatment.
- The operator cannot skip the banner-rise animation while it is in progress, keeping shared live ceremonies synchronized around the reveal.
- Existing shared Firebase ceremony control and restart/replay behavior are preserved.


## V6.4.6 — NMLB Race Control

- Refreshed Pennant Race HQ into NMLB Race Control with a stronger purple postseason-race presentation.
- Restored postseason status markers and added Wild Card E# visibility.
- Fixed completed-season tiebreak elimination: tied W-L clubs now use the standings run-differential tiebreak once both schedules are complete.
- Added a separate Division Out state so division elimination does not incorrectly mean postseason elimination.
- Division-elimination updates now feed Dashboard Race Control, News and the living postseason aftermath desk.
- Postseason `e` remains reserved for clubs mathematically eliminated from every playoff path.


## V6.4.8 — NMLB Pennant Race Command

Pennant Race / Race Control received a full NMLB presentation pass for the Season 4 stretch run. Division standings now display earned `z`, `y`, `x`, `w`, and `e` status markers directly beside team abbreviations, while division-only elimination remains a separate `DIV OUT` state so postseason elimination keeps its precise meaning. Division cards now include live race meters, stronger clinched/eliminated states, refreshed AL/NL mastheads, a sticky NMLB clinching key, an emphasized Wild Card cut line, and a more dramatic postseason command-center visual hierarchy.

## V6.4.8 — Postseason Atmosphere & Memory
- Championship ceremonies now carry continuous team-color confetti from the opening, then ramp to a heavy continuous storm after the banner reaches the rafters.
- Banner landing fires a separate 320-piece impact burst; fireworks now play through the ongoing confetti atmosphere.
- Pennant Race elimination language is simplified: gray `E` means division eliminated, red `E` means postseason eliminated.
- Added postseason series memory, series ratings, turning-point context, team postseason mythology, rivalry temperature, historical comparisons, analyst prediction reputation, memory cards, and NMLB Postseason Tonight coverage.
- Dashboard, News, and Bracket now surface postseason memory generated from every live-scored playoff final, giving non-anchor clubs the same cultural footprint as the favorite club.
- Ceremony completions preserve the speech/fan script used for that event so the ceremony itself becomes part of league history.
