# V4 — Living Universe

V4 turned Franchise Central from a tracker into a living league presentation.

Major systems include league intelligence, magic/elimination numbers, home/away splits, SOS, playoff projections, momentum, personality cards, Banner Room/Trophy Map, Cinderella and dynasty tracking, broadcast themes, win-probability-style game state, leverage, crowd momentum, no-hit watches, player cards, ceremonies, Hall of Fame voting, season packages, playoff/World Series intros, predictions, storyline tracking, Instant Classics, custom history/records, and the 8-week / 5-game season rhythm.

The key rule remains: baseball facts come from tracked data; the simulated world is allowed to create opinions, culture, hype and presentation around those facts.
