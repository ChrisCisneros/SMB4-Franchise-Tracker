# SMB4 Franchise Central 4.5 — Fandom

## Season rhythm
The 40-game regular season is treated as **8 weeks of 5 games**.
- Week 1: Opening Day / first impressions
- Week 2: contenders vs pretenders
- Week 3: rivalries and breakout teams
- Week 4: All-Star push; Game 20 is the break
- Week 5: second-half reset
- Week 6: playoff pressure
- Week 7: clinch/desperation coverage
- Week 8: pennant race

## New Fandom hub
- Fan approval, hype, bandwagon and market-heat meters
- Fan mood and ticket/crowd atmosphere
- Dynamic fake fan poll that drifts every few minutes
- Simulated SMB social posts
- Recurring SMB Network analyst personalities and pregame picks
- Rivalry heat
- Fake official Team Store with rotating merchandise drops
- Dynamic championship, streak and retired-number merchandise
- SMB Network morning/midday/pregame/primetime programming
- League-wide mood

## Connected presentation
- Dashboard now shows Fandom pulse, fan poll and a rotating merch promotion.
- News now includes a fan/media pulse strip.
- Fandom is a first-class navigation page rather than an isolated utility.

## Important design rule
The fandom simulation never invents baseball results. It only simulates the culture around real standings, games, history and user-entered events.

Keep your existing `firebase.js` when installing this update.
