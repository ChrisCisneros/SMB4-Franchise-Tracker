# NMLB — Version 6
## The Living League

Version 6 is the major daily-life release. The app now treats the league as a world that continues to have culture, conversation, memory and anticipation even when no game is active.

### V6 centerpiece: Living League Home
- New V6 League Life engine feeding the Dashboard and Fandom.
- Daily edition framing (morning, afternoon, primetime, late night).
- Today in the League briefing with season day, event, pennant-race pressure and league conversation.
- League Chaos and Drama meters based on recent close games, walk-offs, extra innings, streaks and race pressure.
- Previously On recap that combines recent finals with clinch/elimination consequences.
- Weekly Show with Team of the Week, rough-week club, Game of the Week and next-week setup.

### Recurring people and conversations
- Recurring fan characters built from the existing fan-club universe.
- Persistent identities, running jokes, follower counts, reputation and prediction accuracy presentation.
- Fan-to-fan conversation web with tags, replies, questions, receipts and recurring rivalry relationships.
- No replacement of the user-customized social phrase bank.

### City, fanbase and emotional life
- City Baseball Climate with separate Hope and Anxiety scores.
- Mood labels such as Championship Dreaming, Believing, Restless and Gallows Humor.
- Baseball-climate states such as Electric, Watchful, Hostile and White-Knuckle.

### Media ecosystem
- Recurring programming lanes: Around the Diamond, Numbers Game, Bleacher Noise, October Watch and The Night Cap.
- Existing outlets, podcasts and prediction receipts are connected into the V6 presentation layer.
- Clinches and eliminations can remain active story waves rather than disappearing after the standings marker changes.

### Schedule life
- Road Trip / Homestand context from recent games.
- Off-day event aftermath keeps concerts, movies, Fan Fest and other events in the conversation for several league days.
- Meaningful-games board highlights elimination and scoreboard-watch pressure late in the season.

### Ballpark life
- Evolving park-reputation presentation based on tracked home results and walk-offs.
- Home record, park legend and signature-game context are surfaced together.
- Existing unique park geometry remains unchanged.

### Memory and history
- Historical Echo cards connect current streaks, dramatic games and present standings to the growing archive.
- “You Had to Be There” treatment for dramatic recent finals.
- On This Day remains connected to the daily-life presentation.

### Foundation / safety
- Visible app branding restored to Version 6.0.
- V6 persistent fields added safely inside leagueExtras for future watchlists, commissioner notes, fan memory, event traditions and season time capsules.
- Existing calendar, ceremonies, Museum/Hall, News, Fandom, social phrase customizations, standings and live-game systems remain in place.

### Culture amplification
- Dramatic games can receive informal memory names such as walk-off, marathon, one-run and scoreboard-chaos classics.
- Eliminated teams enter Spoiler Mode instead of falling out of the narrative.
- Repeated off-day events can be recognized as new rituals, traditions in progress or established traditions.
- V6 culture awards track the loudest baseball city, most anxious fanbase, fan-account main character, ballpark drama leader and league nonsense index.
- A live Season Time Capsule summarizes the current top team, league mood, main fan character, featured event, hashtag and chaos level.
- Letters From the Stands provide occasional warm long-form fan voice alongside the usual jokes and arguments.

### Newsroom V6
- V6 Daily Edition sits above the existing News desk.
- City Desk compares hope and anxiety around the league.
- Programming Grid turns media shows into recurring properties instead of isolated flavor cards.
- Postseason Aftermath, Spoiler Desk and History Echoes keep major results alive beyond a single headline.

## V6.4.5 — Majestic Championship Ceremonies

Championship ceremonies are now designed as the largest live spectacles in the NMLB.

- World Series ceremonies expand to a 17-beat coronation with a two-part Commissioner address, two-part manager address, season reflection, two waves of fan voices, slow banner presentation, trophy introduction/reveal, ring introduction/reveal, and a full fireworks finale.
- League Championship ceremonies expand to an 11-beat pennant celebration with the Commissioner, fan voices, manager remarks, season reflection, slow banner presentation, team-color confetti, and fireworks while remaining intentionally one tier below the World Series.
- Commissioner, manager, and fan voice banks were expanded substantially so repeated ceremonies have more variety and better flow.
- Banner raises now take roughly eight seconds. The confetti waits until the banner reaches the rafters, then the celebration erupts.
- Confetti now uses the club's actual franchise primary and secondary colors and increases to a 260-piece championship storm for major reveal stages.
- World Series trophy reveals receive a second confetti storm and an expanded fireworks field; the final coronation receives the largest fireworks treatment.
- The operator cannot skip the banner-rise animation while it is in progress, keeping shared live ceremonies synchronized around the reveal.
- Existing shared Firebase ceremony control and restart/replay behavior are preserved.

## V6.6 — The Postseason Push
V6.6 turns the final stretch into an active history chase and gives the two most visited postseason surfaces a dedicated October command layer.

### Record Chase Command
- Team all-time regular-season wins are compared against archived franchise history, with escalation at ten, five, three, one, tie and break points.
- Current teams can be flagged for new single-season highs and meaningful franchise record threats without flooding the archive with ordinary milestones.
- Player record chasing is a separate curated Record Watch list, limited to five players and stored in `leagueExtras.careerRecordWatches`.
- Player watches support a stat, target, holder, custom title and live current total; they never create ordinary milestone-history entries merely because a player is two or four home runs away.
- Record Watch produces reusable newsroom tags such as `WITHIN REACH`, `HISTORY TONIGHT`, `ONE AWAY`, `RECORD TIED`, `RECORD BROKEN`, `NEW FRANCHISE HIGH` and `NEW ALL-TIME RECORD`.

### October surfaces
- Dashboard adds `HISTORY TONIGHT`, a compact feed of the most important active team and player record chases.
- Bracket adds an October history-watch panel alongside the projected/official field and postseason memory.
- Hall of Records adds Record Chase Command so all-time chases, franchise highs and curated player watches are visible in one place.
- News consumes the same record-watch calculations as Dashboard and Bracket, preventing separate tabs from inventing different versions of the same chase.
- Best-record tracking now compares winning percentage against archived seasons, so a 30–7 season can correctly pass a 29–11 season even before the schedule is complete; mathematically secured records receive their own newsroom alert.
- Daily Results becomes the Final Week Bulk Desk: paste regular or extra-inning finals, add innings and notes, mark walk-offs, add rows in batches, and apply the slate in one pass.
- The Final Week Classics Desk surfaces close games, marathons, walk-offs and high-scoring simulation finals for review, with one-click promotion into the News Network.
- Quick Record Editor adds a Final Week Operator Desk with direct links into bulk entry, NMLB Dashboard and live record-watch status.
- Visible branding updates to NMLB V6.6 · The Postseason Push.
- Reworked All-Time Player Records into six ranked boards: HR, Strikeouts, Hits, Stolen Bases, Saves and Pitching Wins.
- Each board features a large current leader plus second- and third-place entries, record status (`SET`, `EXPANDED` or `BROKEN`) and the season attached to each mark.
- Record entry now uses a category-based Top 3 editor instead of repeated browser prompts, while preserving the curated record data separately from ordinary milestones.


## V6.4.13 — Live State Simplification & Postseason Readiness
V6.4.13 removes the dual-authority live-game persistence system and returns Live Scoring to a single source of truth: Firebase Realtime Database.

- `currentGame` is no longer restored from the general local app snapshot.
- Dedicated live-game local backups, revision arbitration, commit IDs, reconnect republishing, and sync traces are removed.
- Old live-game backup/sync-trace keys are cleared automatically.
- Firebase listeners are read-only consumers; only deliberate operator actions write the live game.
- Bulk franchise sync intentionally leaves `currentGame` untouched so it cannot overwrite an active game.
- Local franchise backups continue for non-live data, but live scoring no longer serializes the full app to localStorage on every pitch/play.
- The temporary Copy Sync Trace control is removed from Live Scoring.

This is a stability-first postseason release: one live game, one persisted authority, fewer recovery layers, and less work on every scoring input.

## V6.5 — Final Week & Living League Expansion

V6.5 turns the final stretch of the regular season into a league-wide event while expanding the everyday culture of the NMLB beyond standings and scores.

### Final Week, clinches & the postseason race
- Added a finalized race-event ledger. Division titles, No. 1 seeds, playoff berths, Wild Cards, division elimination and full postseason elimination are recorded as league events when a regular-season final changes the race.
- Division and No. 1 seed clinches are now independent achievements, so a club that earns both can receive both stories instead of the `z` marker hiding a division-title article.
- News promotes persisted clinch/elimination events and also derives current clinch stories from the exact same race math used by Standings.
- Added Final Week Command presentation across Dashboard, Standings, News, NMLB Center and Bracket.
- Added What Changed Tonight, postseason-field progress, high-pressure clubs, postseason readiness, Selection Show readiness and Final Week ambient presentation.
- Race Control now includes division/berth magic numbers, elimination numbers and a Scoreboard Watching strip identifying the rival that matters most to a pressure club.
- Bracket adds a Selection Show readiness board before the field is fully locked.
- NMLB Network changes programming during Weeks 7–8 with Bubble Watch, Clinching Central and NMLB Race Night, then shifts to Morning After, October Board and Postseason Tonight once October begins.
- Awards Race gains a Final Ballot presentation without falsely declaring live leaders as award winners.

### A more inhabited league
- Added League Pulse: current league mood, hottest story, loudest fanbase, pressure club, underdog energy, hottest merchandise and rivalry heat.
- Added recurring fan personas, postgame call-in reactions and fan reaction counters generated from the league state already being tracked.
- Added weekly league rituals including Team of the Week, Moment of the Week, Heartbreak of the Week, Cold Take of the Week and Quote of the Week.
- Expanded the merchandise world with moment tees, replica scorecards, ticket stubs, game posters, commemorative balls and race-event collector pins.
- Added Franchise Scrapbook / artifact browsing so current finals, race events and franchise identity have a place to live outside the schedule.
- Almanac and Fandom now surface current cultural artifacts alongside the permanent historical universe.

### Historical continuity
- Existing weekly Chronicle snapshots continue automatically through Week 7 and the Week 8 regular-season-final checkpoint.
- New race events are persisted in `leagueExtras`, allowing clinches and eliminations to survive later standings changes and become usable by News, Dashboard, Center and future historical systems.
- Visible app branding is updated to V6.5 — Final Week & Living League Expansion.

V6.5 intentionally builds on the V6.4.13 single-source Live Scoring architecture; it does not reintroduce local live-game authority, revision arbitration or reconnect resurrection logic.
