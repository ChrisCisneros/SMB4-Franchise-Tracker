# V4.x — Living World / Culture

## Current release: V4.12 — The Culture Update

V4.5–V4.11 added Fandom, team fan districts, living social feeds, official team/league accounts, Live Watching, Game Day Social, chronological dashboard chatter, large ambient phrase banks, promotions/giveaways, and Ballpark Life.

V4.12 expands that into a shared culture layer:

- one moderator-only **League Office** instead of scattered admin tabs
- public view-only 40-game promotion calendar
- reseller/collector culture and simulated giveaway secondary market
- parking, gates, seats, vendors, local businesses, watch parties, transit, uniforms, autograph hunters, season-ticket holders, photographers, mascot/foul-ball/wave/scoreboard chatter
- ballpark identity, fanbase identity, favorite/hated opponents, cult heroes/public enemies
- ticket demand, simulated sellout streaks and road-invasion levels
- simulated account growth/reputation and fan culture surveys
- world moments, trends, memes/hashtags and game-thread archive cards
- shared World Pulse on Dashboard/News/Fandom
- expanded official-team language and anti-repeat social variety
- stricter play ownership so defensive plays (especially inning-ending strikeouts) stay credited to the correct team
- horizontal Hall of Banners & Awards: AL/NL league winners appear as pennants, World Series winners as trophies
- V4.12 GameCast trajectory engine with calculated smooth curve points, clearer visual arcs, smoother grounders and no fake bounce on fly outs

### Moderator controls
Unlock with `SF`, then use **League Office** for operations and culture scheduling. The public Fandom calendar can be browsed but not edited.

### League identity
`officialLeagueName` and `officialLeagueAbbr` remain configurable so the temporary Super Mega Baseball League / SMBL name can be replaced later.
